2. Scope of service

SmokingTracker is a cloud-based SaaS platform enabling outpatient substance use disorder treatment centers, counseling practices, and Intensive Outpatient Programs (IOPs) to invite clients to friction-free real-time tracking of cannabis and tobacco consumption, cravings, mood, and contextual triggers, for the purpose of clinical follow-up, Measurement-Based Care, and client retention.

Platform access is provided via a web browser. No app store download is required for clients or staff. The Supplier reserves the right to update platform functionality on an ongoing basis and will communicate material changes by email.

3. Free trial

New customers receive 14 days of free full-access from the activation date. No payment information is required to start the trial. No automatic charge is made at the end of the trial period. The Customer must actively choose a plan and provide payment details to continue after the trial.

The Customer may cancel at any time before the trial ends with no cost and no questions asked by contacting support@smokingtracker.com.

4. Pricing and billing

Subscription pricing is as follows (USD, billed monthly unless the Customer selects annual billing):

  • Solo Counselor — $79/month ($790/year billed annually). 1 counselor seat, unlimited active clients.
  • Group Practice — $249/month ($2,490/year billed annually). Up to 5 counselor seats and 1 clinical director dashboard.
  • Enterprise & Telehealth — Custom pricing. Unlimited seats, EHR integration support, API access, and HIPAA BAA. Contact support@smokingtracker.com for a quote.

An "active client" is a client who has logged at least one session in the billing month.

Billing is monthly in advance. The Supplier issues a PDF invoice by email. Payment is due within 14 days of the invoice date. The Supplier may also offer credit card payment via a secure payment processor.

The Supplier may adjust prices with 60 days' written notice by email. Price changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle after the notice period.

Upon termination, all Customer and client data is deleted within 30 days of the agreement end date, unless the Customer has requested a data export or otherwise agreed in writing.
