Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Questions from treatment centers, answered.

Getting Started

How quickly can we get started?
Most treatment centers are up and running within one working day. Once you sign up, you'll receive a setup link by email. From there, you can invite your first citizen within minutes.
Do citizens need to download an app?
No. SmokingTracker runs entirely in the browser. Citizens can access it from any smartphone, tablet, or computer — no app store required.

Pricing & Billing

What does it cost?
SmokingTracker is invoiced per active citizen per month. The Standard plan (1 treatment center) starts at 2,000 DKK/month + 40 DKK per active citizen. Multi-site (unlimited centers) is 3,000 DKK/month + 35 DKK per active citizen. All plans include a 14-day free trial.
How is billing handled?
We invoice via EAN or PDF invoice — the standard method for Danish municipalities. No automatic credit card charges. After the 14-day trial, you'll receive an invoice.
Can we cancel the trial?
Yes. You can cancel at any time before the 14-day trial ends — no questions asked, no invoice sent.

Privacy & Security

Who owns the citizen's data?
The citizen does. They choose exactly what to share through granular consent toggles and can withdraw consent or request deletion at any time.
Is SmokingTracker GDPR-compliant?
Yes. SmokingTracker is built Privacy by Design. We act as a data processor under your controller role. A full Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is included with all plans. All data is stored in the EU.
Where is the data stored?
All data is stored on EU-based servers. We never transfer data outside the EU/EEA.

Technical

Does it integrate with KMD Nexus or Cura?
Directly, no — but you can export one-click PDF reports ready to attach to case files in KMD Nexus, Cura, or any other case management system.
What browsers and devices are supported?
All modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) on smartphones, tablets, and computers. No installation required for citizens or staff.

For Clinicians

What does the app track — cigarettes or cannabis?
Cannabis. The app supports joint, vaporizer, vape pen, edible, and concentrate — the citizen chooses their method during onboarding. The name SmokingTracker is historical; the platform is built specifically for cannabis addiction treatment.
What exactly triggers green, yellow, and red?
The traffic light is based on activity — how recently the citizen logged a session. Green: logged within the last 2 days. Yellow: last log was 2–7 days ago. Red: no log in 7+ days, or never logged. It reflects engagement with the app, not the quantity consumed.
What happens when a citizen withdraws consent?
Their data is hidden from you immediately — not deleted. You can still see their alias, traffic light status, and last activity timestamp. The citizen can reactivate consent at any time, and their full history will be visible again.
What happens in a crisis situation?
You set custom trigger rules per citizen — for example 'alert me after 5 days of inactivity' or 'if urge frequency increases by 50% week-over-week.' When a rule fires, you receive a notification via app, email, or SMS. SmokingTracker does not replace crisis intervention — it gives you the early signals to act before things escalate.
Who is the data controller?
Azlo is the data controller for the SmokingTracker platform. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is included with all plans and sets out the responsibilities of both parties. Contact us at support@smokingtracker.com with any data protection questions.
What about citizens without a smartphone?
SmokingTracker is a web app — no download required. It runs in any modern browser on any device: smartphone, tablet, or computer. Citizens only need internet access.
