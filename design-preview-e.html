The recall problem

Cannabis affects short-term memory. Your clients aren't lying — they just can't recall.

Regular cannabis use impairs episodic memory encoding. By the time a client sits across from you, a full week of sessions, moods, and triggers has dissolved into a vague "about the same." Ecological Momentary Assessment fixes this — not by asking them to remember, but by capturing it the moment it happens.
