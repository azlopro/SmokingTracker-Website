Why counselors use SmokingTracker

Consistent Client Self-Monitoring

Three taps to log a session — low enough friction that clients actually do it between appointments. Consistent self-monitoring gives you the behavior data you need for effective CBT interventions.

Structured Support Between Sessions

SmokingTracker provides structured support to clients between appointments — filling the gap where most treatment programs have nothing. Counselors reclaim session time for intervention rather than recall reconstruction.

Three Treatment Goal Modes

Reduce, Quit, or Awareness mode — aligning with harm reduction, moderation management, and abstinence approaches. Clients set their own goal. Counselors see progress toward it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do individual users pay?

Individual users pay $1/month — a symbolic commitment to their own recovery journey. If that's out of reach, free access is available with no questions asked. Apply here.

Are there long-term contracts?

No. You can cancel your subscription at any time. If you cancel, you will still have access to your dashboard until the end of your current billing period.

How does the free trial work?

You get full access to all features in your chosen plan for 14 days. No credit card is required to start. At the end of the trial, you can choose to subscribe to keep your access.
