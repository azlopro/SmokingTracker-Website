# Pricing — SmokingTracker

SmokingTracker is a cannabis consumption tracking platform for outpatient treatment centers and cannabis counselors.
All plans include a 14-day free trial. No automatic payments without consent.

## Solo Practitioner

- Price: $79/month | $65/month (billed $790/year — save 20%)
- Seats: 1 therapist
- Clients: Unlimited active clients
- Features:
  - Real-time cannabis consumption tracking
  - Free patient mobile app (iOS + Android)
  - Traffic light clinical dashboard
  - Clinical session reports (exportable)
  - Full GDPR compliance + Data Processing Agreement (DPA) included
- Trial: 14-day free trial, no credit card required
- Signup: https://smokingtracker.com/pricing.html

## Group Practice

- Price: $249/month | $207/month (billed $2,490/year — save 20%)
- Seats: Up to 5 counselor seats + 1 clinical director dashboard
- Clients: Unlimited active clients
- Features:
  - Everything in Solo Practitioner
  - Multi-practitioner coordination
  - Clinical director oversight dashboard
  - Centralized billing for the practice
  - Aggregate reporting across all counselors
- Trial: 14-day free trial
- Signup: https://smokingtracker.com/pricing.html

## Enterprise & Telehealth

- Price: Custom — contact for quote
- Seats: Unlimited
- Clients: Unlimited
- Features:
  - Everything in Group Practice
  - EHR integration
  - API access
  - Dedicated account support
  - HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

- Target: Large treatment networks, multi-site clinics, telehealth platforms
- Contact: https://smokingtracker.com/trial.html

## Notes

- All prices in USD
- Annual billing offers 20% discount vs. monthly
