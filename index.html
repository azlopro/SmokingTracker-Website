Everything Your Team Needs

SmokingTracker bridges the gap between the citizen's daily reality and your clinical workflow — without compromising privacy.

Privacy by Design

The citizen owns their data. Granular consent toggles let them choose what to share: session history, urge data, mood logs, or notes. Trust-first design means higher engagement.

Traffic Light Dashboard

See all your citizens at a glance. Green is active, Yellow needs attention, Red is inactive. Click through for week-over-week comparisons, trend charts, calendars, and full session breakdowns.

Smart Trigger Alarms

Set custom rules per citizen: 'alert me after 3 consecutive smoking days' or 'if inactive for 5 days.' Receive notifications in-app, by email, or via SMS.

One-Click Clinical Reports

Open a print-ready report in one click — save as PDF or print directly. All shared data — sessions, trends, and consent status — ready to attach in KMD Nexus or Cura.

Built-In Urge Surfing

When a citizen feels a craving, the app guides them through a 3-minute breathing exercise with distractions. If they resist, they earn achievements. If not, the session is logged without judgment.

Achievements & Motivation

Gamified milestones reward consistency: smoke-free days, reduction targets, logging streaks. The citizen stays motivated without clinical pressure.

Three Treatment Goal Modes

Each citizen picks a goal that matches where they are: Reduce (daily session budget), Quit (smoke-free timer, cold turkey or gradual), or Awareness (just track, no pressure). The app adapts its entire experience to their goal.

How It Works

Three steps from onboarding to measurable results — no technical requirements for the citizen.

01

Invite the Citizen

Create an invitation from the Behandler Dashboard. The citizen receives a link via email, SMS, or a manual handoff — whatever fits. No app downloads required.

02

They Track at Their Own Pace

The citizen logs sessions, moods, and locations when they want. Built-in Urge Surfing exercises help them resist cravings. They choose exactly what to share with you through granular consent toggles.

03

You Get Actionable Insights

Your dashboard shows real-time traffic lights, weekly comparisons, trend charts, and trigger alarms. Export PDF reports in one click for KMD Nexus, Cura, or case conferences.
