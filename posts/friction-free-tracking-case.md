For most substance abuse treatment centers, retaining citizens in a program is the biggest challenge. Especially citizens who are referred against their will — whether by the municipality, the courts, or family members — often drop out within the first few weeks.

But what if the solution isn't to push harder, but to take a step back?

## The Problem With the Traditional Approach

Most treatment programs require the citizen to declare willingness to stop or reduce. It sounds reasonable, but creates a binary situation: either you're "ready," or you're out.

For citizens who didn't choose to be there, it's a guaranteed recipe for early dropout. They feel pressured, lose ownership, and shut down.

## The Pilot: "Just Track"

In the autumn of 2025, a treatment center in Central Jutland offered SmokingTracker to 23 newly enrolled citizens, all categorized as "low motivation" or "involuntarily referred."

The approach was radically different:

> "We're not asking you to stop. We're not even asking you to cut down. We just want you to log what you're already doing."

Citizens got SmokingTracker installed with a single consumption method and one instruction: log your smoking session when you smoke. No goals, no limits, no pressure.

## The Results

After 4 weeks, the numbers were striking:

- **87%** were still actively using the app (compared to the normal ~55% retention)
- **61%** had voluntarily requested to share additional data with their counselor
- **35%** had started setting their own reduction goals — completely voluntarily

### What Happened?

The simple act of tracking created **awareness**. Citizens started noticing patterns they'd never paid attention to:

- "I always smoke twice as much on Fridays"
- "When I'm stressed at work, I smoked 2 more joints than usual"
- "I thought it was 3-4 joints a day — it's actually 7-8"

This self-insight is the foundation for any behavioral change. And most importantly: it came from within — not as an external demand.

## What This Means for Your Center

SmokingTracker's Privacy by Design model is built precisely for this situation:

1. **The citizen controls everything**: They choose what to share with the counselor via granular consent toggles
2. **No judgment**: The app logs without moralizing. There are no "you failed" messages
3. **Urge Surfing**: When citizens feel an urge, the app guides them through a 3-minute wave exercise — regardless of whether they give in or not
4. **Counselors see only what's shared**: The traffic light dashboard shows activity level, not details, unless the citizen has given consent

## Conclusion

Retention isn't about pushing harder. It's about meeting citizens where they are — and giving them a tool that rewards awareness over perfection.
